# Question bank sources

This file is the verification log for `questions.json`. The game is only fair if every "right answer" is right, so every quote in the bank has been checked against a real source. If you edit the bank, keep these rules and add a row here for anything you add:

- **Translation.** Bible quotes are King James Version (KJV), word for word. You may drop a leading "And", "For" or "But", drop end punctuation (end every quote with a full stop when the verse is cut mid-sentence, so Bible options do not stand out by missing punctuation), quote part of a verse, and capitalise the first letter of a quote that starts mid-verse. Never change, modernise or reorder words inside the quoted span. An ellipsis is allowed only at the very start or end. Keep KJV spelling ("goeth", "an haughty").
- **Sources.** Bible `source` is `Book Chapter:Verse (KJV)`. Non-Bible `source` names the real person or work, title or context, and year if known. If an origin is uncertain, say so in `source`; never invent an attribution. Only Bible sources end in "(KJV)".
- **Decoys.** Proverbs, philosophers, literature, hymn writers, preachers, famous people, and sayings that sound biblical but are not. A misquote of a real verse is a fair decoy only if its exact wording is not in the KJV (as a whole verse or any part of one), and the note must give the real verse. No scripture of other religions and nothing from the Apocrypha. Nothing crude or mocking.
- **Limits.** Quotes at most 160 characters, notes at most 140, no duplicate quotes, 40-60% of yes/no items are Bible. `test/questions.test.js` checks the mechanical rules; this file is the evidence for the content.

## How items were checked

- **Bible quotes:** each verse was fetched from Bible Gateway (KJV) and compared word for word. Every quote was also checked by script as an exact substring of the verse in two independent public-domain KJV datasets ([thiagobodruk/bible `en_kjv.json`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thiagobodruk/bible/master/json/en_kjv.json) and [aruljohn/Bible-kjv](https://github.com/aruljohn/Bible-kjv)).
- **Non-Bible quotes:** the origin was checked at the linked source, and the normalised wording was searched across the full KJV text (all 66 books, including across verse boundaries) to confirm it does not appear.
- One candidate was dropped during checking: "Money is the root of all evil" is itself a contiguous run of words inside 1 Timothy 6:10 ("For the love of money is the root of all evil"), so it is not a fair "not in the Bible" answer.
